Workspace and the first three templates

Each frame below is the live workspace, not a picture. Type in a field and the resume updates. Hover the resume to see what each part maps to, and click it to jump to that field. Focusing a field highlights its element on the page.

Every printed element carries a data-path taken from the schema (for example work.0.highlights.1). That one attribute drives the hover outline, click to edit, form-to-preview highlight and the error markers.

Flags on UX.md and the schema
  1. Summary is a section key but lives in basics.summary. The brief puts it inside Basics; I gave it its own card so it can be reordered, hidden and renamed like the others. It still writes to basics.summary.
  2. Profiles have no section key. Meridian prints them as “Find me online”, but they can't be reordered, hidden or renamed through preferences. Suggest adding profiles to sectionKey.
  3. sectionOrder can't move a section across columns. In two-column templates, dragging Projects above Experience does nothing visible. The Content list marks sidebar sections with a “Sidebar” tag so the order still makes sense.
  4. x-notes isn't in the schema. It's a convention between our AI prompt and the review banner. The prompt has to define it (root-level array of strings) or the banner stays empty.
  5. Banner position. UX.md sketches the review banner and problems panel at the bottom. I put the banner above the page (seen first after an import) and the problems panel at the top of the left panel, next to the fields it points to.
  6. Unknown keys after import. basics.emial is listed with its value and a “Go to email” link, so retyping is a paste, not a memory test.
  7. One page, always. Width is fixed (A4 210 mm or Letter 8.5 in) and the height grows to fit, with A4/Letter height as the minimum. The PDF gets a single page sized to match (@page { size: 210mm <height>mm }). The Fit check warns past 130% of standard height, where printing on normal paper shrinks the text. This replaces the brief's multi-page and page-2 rules. Academic CV is dropped; the set is 9 single-page resume templates.
