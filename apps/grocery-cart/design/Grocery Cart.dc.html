Grocery Cart

Turns a typed grocery list into a filled JioMart cart, then stops. Part A is the local web app (1280 px, down to 900 px). Part B is the later Chrome side-panel extension, derived from A. Light and dark frames are mixed throughout.

B · Browser extension (later)

Chrome side panel, 400 px wide, next to the JioMart tab. Remembered choices opens as a full tab reusing A7.

B1First run
Grocery Cart

Type a list. Get a filled JioMart cart.

  1. 01
    Only your own JioMart tab.
    It works in the JioMart tab where you're already signed in. Nothing else.
  2. 02
    You review every match.
    Swap products, change pack counts or skip lines before anything is added.
  3. 03
    It never checks out.
    It stops at a full cart. You check it and pay on JioMart yourself.
